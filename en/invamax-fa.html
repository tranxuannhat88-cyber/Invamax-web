INVAMAX FA™ — FACTORY CAPABILITY ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

Assess capability accurately. Identify gaps clearly.
Determine priorities correctly.

INVAMAX Factory Assessment™ is INVAMAX's comprehensive methodology for assessing factory operational capability.

Instead of just inspecting isolated issues, the methodology assesses the factory as a complete operating system, from governance foundation to production, data, technology, and continuous improvement capability.

The goal is to answer three questions:

Where is the factory currently? What capabilities are missing? What changes need to be prioritized?

What does INVAMAX Factory Assessment™ evaluate?

Governance Foundation

Evaluate the clarity and effectiveness of the enterprise management foundation.

  • • Strategy and objectives
  • • Organizational structure and responsibilities
  • • People and team capabilities
  • • Standard work

Factory Operations

Evaluate the ability to convert customer demand into products stably, quickly, and efficiently.

  • • Production flow and factory layout
  • • Capabilities and production planning
  • • Daily management
  • • Quality
  • • Productivity, inventory, delivery, and bottlenecks

Knowledge, Data, and Digitalization

Evaluate the ability to use information and technology to support operations.

  • • Knowledge management
  • • Ability to collect and utilize data
  • • Level of visual management and information transparency
  • • Digitalization, automation, and AI application

Continuous Improvement and Sustainment

Evaluate the ability to self-detect issues, solve them, and upgrade the system over time.

  • • Problem solving
  • • Kaizen and continuous improvement
  • • Standardization after improvement
  • • Ability to sustain the system

Value Delivered to the Enterprise

A comprehensive picture of current capabilities

Leadership can see where the factory is strong, where it is weak, and what points are limiting efficiency or growth potential.

Identify capability gaps

Compare current capabilities with required capabilities to meet business goals or the desired operating model.

Determine the right improvement priorities

Do not try to improve everything at once. INVAMAX helps identify issues and capabilities that need priority because they have the greatest impact on results.

Basis for restructuring and investment decisions

The assessment results provide leaders with a basis when deciding on:
Restructuring Expansion Equipment investment Reorganization Digitalization Operating model transformation

Basis for assessing suppliers or investment targets

The methodology can also be used to objectively assess a factory's operational capability before ordering, partnering, increasing volume, or investing.

Formulate a factory upgrade roadmap

The final output is not just a score or assessment report, but:
Current state Capability gap Priorities Direction for improvement Transformation roadmap

From current state assessment to actionable decisions

INVAMAX Factory Assessment™ helps transform what is happening on the shop floor into a structured picture so leaders know clearly: where they are, where they need to go, and where to start making changes.
