FACTORY CAPABILITY ASSESSMENT

Understand current capabilities correctly.
Make the right decisions for the next step.

INVAMAX helps businesses comprehensively assess factory operational capabilities, clearly identifying strengths, weaknesses, risks, and capability gaps as a basis for decisions on transformation, expansion, supplier selection, or investment.

When is a factory capability assessment needed?

Internal assessment before transformation or expansion

Identify where the factory is strong, where it is weak, and which capabilities need to be upgraded before restructuring, investing, or scaling up.

Need an independent party to assess your factory

The leadership team needs an objective external perspective to validate internal assessments and identify issues the organization may be accustomed to or find hard to see.

Supplier capability assessment

Verify actual capabilities in production, quality, delivery, management, and responsiveness before selecting, ordering, or increasing production volume.

Assessment before investment or partnership

Determine the operational capability, risks, and upgrade potential of a factory before contributing capital, acquiring, joint venturing, or establishing long-term partnerships.

Why choose INVAMAX?

Independent and objective

Assessment based on current status, data, and factual evidence, minimizing the influence of internal interests or one-sided information.

Real factory operations experience

INVAMAX assesses from the perspective of someone who understands how a factory actually operates — from organization, planning, production, and quality to daily management.

Lean manufacturing management foundation

Lean thinking helps identify waste, bottlenecks, imbalances, and root causes that degrade productivity, quality, speed, and resource utilization efficiency.

Comprehensive systemic assessment

Not just looking at machinery or capacity, INVAMAX assesses the entire operating system:

Organization → People → Workflow → Capabilities → Planning → Quality → Data → Technology → Improvement

Assessment to support decision making

Results are presented to help decision-makers answer clearly:

Should we transform? Do we have the capability to expand? Should we place an order? Should we partner or invest?

Identify gaps and improvement directions

INVAMAX not only points out strengths and weaknesses but also clarifies:

Current Capabilities → Required Capabilities → Gaps → Priority Level → Improvement Direction

A good assessment must lead to a good decision.

If your business needs to clearly understand the actual capabilities of a factory before transformation, expansion, ordering, partnering, or investing, start with a discussion with INVAMAX.
