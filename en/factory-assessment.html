01. OPERATIONAL PAIN POINTS

Why Do Factories Need Digital Operations?

As plant scale and product complexity increase, conventional manual methods break down.

Information Fragmentation

Data is scattered across fragmented spreadsheets, paper forms, and isolated tools, making consolidation nearly impossible.

Lack of Operational Visibility

Blind spots in tracking order status, job progress, shopfloor output, and recurring operational bottlenecks.

Manual, Paper-Heavy Workflows

Duplicate data entry, time-consuming static reporting, and friction across functional departments.

Delayed Decision-Making

Lack of real-time visualizations and operational indicators impedes timely, high-impact decisions.

💡

Digitalization is not simply entering data into software; it is fundamentally upgrading how your plant organizes work, connects shopfloor data, and governs operations.
