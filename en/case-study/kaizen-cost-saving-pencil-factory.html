Improvement value does not come from a single large project.

Over 4 years, the factory continuously addressed various forms of waste: redundant manpower, waiting time, transportation, suboptimal layouts, material consumption, downtime, and unnecessary tasks.

When improvements are measured, standardized, and replicated, small changes on the shop floor accumulate into significant value for the entire system.
