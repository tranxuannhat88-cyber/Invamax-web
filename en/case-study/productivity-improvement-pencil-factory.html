The Problem

At a pencil manufacturing plant, productivity is not only limited by the workers' speed but also by multiple factors in the operating system: management structure, resource allocation, lost time during shifts, bottlenecks between stages, layout, and production flow.

Therefore, the goal is not to demand workers to work faster, but to:

"Eliminate factors that make one labor hour generate less value than the system's actual capacity."

System Improvement Over Task Improvement

01

Production
Organization

Reorganize stage management and increase resource coordination capacity.

02

Manpower &
Capacity

Measure actual productivity, control labor hours and deploy personnel based on demand.

03

Flow

Improve layout, resolve bottlenecks and shorten the production flow.

04

Waste
Elimination

Reduce waiting, transportation, motion and non-value-adding tasks.

05

Standard &
Kaizen

Standardize effective solutions and replicate them across other stages.

06

Digital

Digitize shop-floor production data to support Daily Management.
