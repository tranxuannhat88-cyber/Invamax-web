INVAMAX LAUNCHES FRACTIONAL COO PARTNERSHIP PROGRAM

Flexible factory operations leadership – Elevating operational performance – Building sustainable management capability

INVAMAX is a pioneering unit in Vietnam introducing the Fractional COO Partnership Program, directly partnering with manufacturing enterprises to manage, restructure, and upgrade factory management systems through a flexible operating model.

Schedule a Consultation with INVAMAX Explore Factory Operations Solutions

During growth and expansion, many manufacturing enterprises face increasingly complex challenges in factory management and daily execution.

Production scale grows, yet management systems fail to keep pace. Business owners must personally resolve numerous daily operational issues. Cross-departmental coordination lacks alignment, productivity remains unstable, costs escalate, and the internal management team is not yet equipped to meet the demands of growth.

Meanwhile, recruiting and retaining a full-time Factory Chief Operating Officer (COO) with the right capabilities, hands-on experience, and cultural fit is not always the optimal choice.

To help manufacturing enterprises overcome these challenges, INVAMAX is a pioneering provider in Vietnam launching the Fractional COO Partnership Program – Flexible Factory Operations Leadership.

WHAT IS A FRACTIONAL COO?

A Fractional COO (Fractional Chief Operating Officer) is a partnership model in which an enterprise engages executive-level operational leadership based on agreed timelines, scope of work, and performance objectives, rather than hiring a full-time COO.

In Vietnam, INVAMAX is a pioneer in deploying the Fractional COO partnership program specifically tailored for manufacturing plants. Unlike traditional consulting engagements that focus solely on diagnosis and recommendations, INVAMAX’s Fractional COO program emphasizes direct participation in factory operations management, improvement implementation, and management team capability development.

The goal is not merely to solve immediate operational bottlenecks, but to build a management system capable of sustaining performance and driving continuous improvement.

WHO IS THIS PROGRAM FOR?

The program is tailored for small and medium-sized manufacturing enterprises (SMEs), particularly factories that:

  • Employ around 50 or more workers and seek to elevate overall operational efficiency.
  • Are scaling production capacity while management systems have not yet kept pace.
  • Rely heavily on the business owner or a few key individuals for daily operations.
  • Face ongoing challenges in productivity, quality, delivery lead times, costs, and inventory.
  • Lack a middle-management team capable of coordinating, controlling, and executing plans effectively.
  • Want to upgrade their factory operating system without needing a full-time COO hire.
Workforce size serves as a reference guideline rather than a strict requirement. INVAMAX evaluates partnership feasibility based on each enterprise's actual needs and operational reality.

CORE PROGRAM COMPONENTS

The partnership program focuses on four core pillars:

01

1. Current State Assessment & Goal Setting

Evaluate the existing management system, organizational structure, production operations, operational performance, and team capabilities.

Identify root-cause bottlenecks and align on clear objectives, KPIs, and an improvement roadmap.

02

2. Direct Factory Operations Leadership

Within the authority delegated by the enterprise, the Fractional COO coordinates cross-functional operations, organizes plan execution, monitors results, and resolves critical operational issues.

Focused squarely on productivity, quality, cost, on-time delivery, and resource utilization.

03

3. Management System Building & Team Development

Establish and refine planning systems, KPIs, delegation frameworks, work control mechanisms, reporting routines, and continuous improvement processes.

Simultaneously develop the leadership, coordination, and problem-solving capabilities of the internal management team.

04

4. Standardization, Optimization & Handover

Progressively standardize management methodologies, establish stable operating rhythms, and transfer leadership capability to the internal team.

As the system matures, the partnership model can transition toward reduced on-site presence while maintaining periodic oversight and executive support.

FLEXIBLE PARTNERSHIP MODEL

Depending on each factory’s current state and specific requirements, INVAMAX proposes a phased partnership model:

PHASE 1
Phase 1 – Assessment & Planning

Assess the current operating state, define clear objectives, establish scope, and build the implementation roadmap.

PHASE 2
Phase 2 – Direct Operations Leadership & System Upgrade

INVAMAX engages deeply in daily factory operations, drives improvement initiatives, and upgrades the management system. This phase may involve intensive or full-time engagement depending on complexity.

PHASE 3
Phase 3 – Fractional Executive Accompaniment

Once the operating system and internal management team reach sufficient maturity, the engagement transitions to a scheduled fractional rhythm, combining on-site leadership and remote support as agreed.

PHASE 4
Phase 4 – Evaluation & Handover

Evaluate achieved outcomes, drive further optimization, and progressively hand over full operational ownership to the internal leadership team.

Implementation timelines, engagement frequency, scope of authority, responsibilities, and evaluation metrics are tailored and mutually agreed upon with each enterprise.
