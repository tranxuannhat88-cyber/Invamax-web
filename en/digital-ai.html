AUTOMATION, DIGITALIZATION & AI FOR MANUFACTURING

Reduce manual tasks. Decrease labor dependency.
Increase operational capabilities.

INVAMAX helps manufacturing enterprises apply automation, digitalization, and AI at the exact value-creation points in the factory operating system.

INVAMAX helps businesses apply automation, digitalization, and artificial intelligence to equipment, processes, and production management.

We deploy across 3 distinct layers: Equipment & Machinery Automation, Process & Data Digitalization, and AI for Operations & Decision Support.

The goal is not only to process data faster, but also to reduce manual tasks, decrease the required labor force, increase productivity, enhance stability, and create a smarter factory.

Ultimate Goals

Less manual labor

Machines and systems take over increasingly repetitive, strenuous, or standardizable tasks.

Higher productivity

Increase output with the same level of manpower, equipment, floor space, and production time.

Lower operating costs

Reduce labor costs, redundant movements, defects, rework, waiting time, and non-value-added activities.

Quality management and high stability

Decrease dependency on individual manual operations and increase process control capabilities.

Greater transparency

Production data is updated quickly, visually, and is ready for operational management.

Faster response

Abnormalities are detected early, alerts are sent to the right people, and issues are handled faster.

Start with operational problems,
not with technology

INVAMAX identifies:

Problem to solve Process to improve Task to eliminate Equipment to automate Data to digitalize AI to assist

The goal is to choose the right level of automation and digitalization to create real economic efficiency, rather than investing in technology just for the sake of modernization.

Step-by-step building of a new generation factory

  • Humans do tasks requiring thinking and decision-making.
  • Machines do tasks that are repetitive and standardizable.
  • Digital systems manage the flow of information.
  • AI assists in analysis and decision-making.
