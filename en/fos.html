INVAMAX FOS™ — FACTORY MANAGEMENT OPERATING SYSTEM

Transforming Factory Operations into a Unified System

INVAMAX FOS™ (Factory Operating System) is a factory management operating system developed by INVAMAX,
connecting management, production, knowledge, tools, and improvement activities into a seamless operating system.

Structure of INVAMAX FOS™

INVAMAX FOS™ organizes the core capabilities of a factory into a common operating architecture consisting of 4 groups and 11 interconnected modules.

01

Management Foundation

Directs how the system is organized and managed.

02

Production Operations

Translates goals into actual daily activities.

03

Knowledge & Tools

Retains knowledge in the system and supports operations with appropriate tools.

04

Improvement & Sustenance

Helps the system solve problems and continue to mature.

FOS doesn't replace the management team. FOS creates a system so the management team can operate the factory more consistently.

Map of the 11 Modules of INVAMAX FOS™

ONE SYSTEM · 4 CAPABILITY GROUPS · 11 INTERCONNECTED MODULES

11 modules are organized into 4 groups, but they operate as one unified system.

Group 1 — Management Foundation

01. Core

Strategy & Operating Mechanism

Connects corporate objectives & KPIs with governance, clear delegation, review cadences, and risk management.

02. People

People & Organizational Capability

Organizational structure, role accountability, competency development, leadership culture, and succession pipelines.

03. Flow

End-to-End Operational Architecture

End-to-end order-to-delivery value streams, workflow, material and information flow, cross-department interfaces, and customer experience.

Group 2 — Production Operations

04. Standard

Standard Work & Standards

Standard Work instructions, SOPs & checklists, visual management, compliance auditing, and management of change (MOC).

05. Capacity

Capacity & Line Balancing

Takt time & throughput capacity, line balancing, machinery management, capacity planning, and downtime control.

06. Daily

Daily Shopfloor Management

Daily shift plans, SQDCP visual tracking, rapid variance detection & escalation, standup huddles, and disciplined action tracking.

07. Quality

Quality at the Source

In-process quality gates, proactive defect prevention, Poka-Yoke mistake proofing, root-cause CAPA, and full product traceability.

Group 3 — Knowledge & Tools

08. Knowledge

Knowledge Management

Centralized SOP repository, lessons learned documentation, internal knowledge transfer, and intellectual property protection.

09. Digital

Digital Systems & Data

Accurate data capture, digitized workflows, real-time executive dashboards & reports, telemetry alerts, and targeted AI applications.

Group 4 — Improvement & Sustenance

10. Kaizen

Problem Solving & Continuous Improvement

Agile problem identification, 5-Why root-cause analysis, countermeasure implementation, KPI impact auditing, and standardized results.

11. Sustain

Sustain & Operational Maturity

Periodic process audits & maturity assessments, change management, skill reinforcement, compliance discipline, and ongoing operating system evolution.

Where Does the Value of INVAMAX FOS™ Lie?

A Unified Operating Architecture

Strategy, people, process, data, and improvement operate within the same logic.

End-to-End Connection Instead of Local Optimization

Departments do not just optimize their own tasks but serve the common operational flow together.

Reduced Reliance on Individual Methods

Operating methods are built into the system, standards, and management mechanisms.

Standardized Yet Flexible

FOS creates a common architecture but can be adjusted to the specific characteristics of each factory.

Laying the Groundwork for Lean, Digital & AI

Improvement and technology are placed in the right systemic positions rather than implemented disjointedly.

FOS is the system layer sitting between the Target Operating Model and the actual daily operations of the factory.

Is Your Factory Being Run by a System — or by the Experience of the People There?

INVAMAX FOS™ helps connect disjointed management and operational activities into a structured, seamless, and continuously developing system.
